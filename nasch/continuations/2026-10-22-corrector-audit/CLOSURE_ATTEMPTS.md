# Closure audit: what the new results do and do not supply

This record concerns the reduced continuous-time backchip chain. The target
`lim_N q_(N,floor(eta*N))`, `0 < eta < 2`, remains OPEN. None of the arguments
below licenses interchanging the rare-braking and thermodynamic limits of
the original traffic process.

## The finite-size comparison route

The preceding continuation proves that an upper comparison of q under
doubling and tripling, with error `C_R/N`, implies convergence at every fixed
density along all road sizes. The present Lemma 1 allows either ordinary
population `kH` or corrected population `k(H-1)+1`.

Theorem 4 disproves a particular proposed way to establish this comparison:
sort the folded height profiles and average their exact small-ring current
correctors. The defect is of order one for every odd small-ring size. This
is a generator counterexample at specified configurations. It is not a
comparison of their stationary weights, and does not disprove the desired
stationary inequality.

Cyclic envelopes and fractional offsets were also evaluated. The finite
outputs are retained separately. No uniform bound for these candidates was
proved. In particular, failure of one pointwise lower bound does not rule
out a bound on its stationary average.

## The time-versus-size route

The monotone corrector supplies uniform control of its compensated noise:
`E M_t^2 <= F^2*t` and the exponential bound in Corollary 2.1. This is a
proved consequence of nonnegative corrector jumps whose sum is F at every
configuration, not an assumption of independent particle motion.

However, the correction to current through a fixed bond has exact oscillation
`H*(N-1)/N`. The two endpoint configurations giving this oscillation are
rotations of a single pile containing all H units. Thus a worst-case
sublinear correction cannot follow from this finite-ring corrector. A
stationary concentration or mean-control argument would still be required.

## The marked-queue route

The previously proved tagged-queue representation was reconsidered because
it naturally accounts for the lost unit in the corrected joining conjecture.
Its input bit is `1{sum(X) < H-1}`. Changing the road size changes both the
exterior vector and this feedback threshold. Componentwise order of the
visible exterior coordinates alone does not order the input bits. No
stationary joining with the required comparison was constructed.

The marked exiting-label representation and all its earlier exact identities
remain available. Neither convergence of the scaled exit rank nor preservation
of mass intensity through arbitrarily long tandems was established in this run.

## General-theorem check

Gobron and Saada, *Couplings, Attractiveness and Hydrodynamics for Conservative
Particle Systems*, [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0903.0316), Theorem
5.13, treats a compact local state space in its first case and assumes a
family of product invariant measures in its second case. Neither alternative
supplies the missing canonical-limit result for the present model. The
unbounded stick-process example has additional structure; allowing multiple
particles to jump is not by itself enough to apply its conclusion here.

Fajfrova, Gobron and Saada, *Invariant measures of Mass Migration Processes*,
[author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.00778), provides general
product-measure and attractiveness results. It does not establish the
needed stationary product family for this backchip rule. No applicable
general theorem closing the present gap was identified in this check; this
is not a claim of exhaustive literature coverage.

## Remaining mathematical obligation

Prove a valid all-size stationary comparison, or prove the missing canonical
mass-retention and phase-selection estimates, or establish a different
argument fixing q independently of those two issues. Moving condensates and
phase mixtures remain unexcluded. No stationary counterexample to convergence
was obtained. The published statements in this continuation are internally
audited mathematical arguments, not independent peer review.
